Osmolal & anion gap calculator

Calculate serum gaps with explicit units. Calculations run locally in your browser; no server, account, or patient identifiers are needed.

Not a poisoning screen or treatment protocol. A normal osmolal or anion gap does not exclude methanol or ethylene glycol poisoning, especially after metabolism. An elevated gap is nonspecific. If exposure is suspected, seek immediate clinical/toxicology assessment and definitive testing.

Laboratory measurements

Chloride and bicarbonate must be supplied together to calculate the anion gap.

Anion gap mEq/L (without potassium; albumin uncorrected)

Formula: 2 × Na + glucose/18 + BUN/2.8 + ethanol/4.6 (ethanol term only when supplied). Osmolal gap = measured osmolality − calculated value. Anion gap = Na − (Cl + HCO₃).

Different laboratories use different osmolarity equations, and assay variability affects the gap. For definitive evaluation use the local laboratory's approach and clinical context.
